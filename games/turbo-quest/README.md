# Turbo Quest

A side-scrolling platformer for a 4-year-old and his grown-up. Turbo is a corgi pup with goggles and a rocket backpack, the Pop-Pack. The Grumblies are grumpy purple blobs with little rain clouds over their heads. Turbo's shots don't hurt them, they **cheer them up**: a cheered Grumbly turns into a smiling balloon and floats away, and you can bounce on the balloon.

No reading needed, and no lives or game over. A pit floats Turbo back in a bubble, and running out of hearts floats him back to the last checkpoint with full hearts.

This is **World 1 (M2)**: four levels on an island map, ending in a boss fight, in both modes, plus a practice field. Between levels there's a Pup Closet to dress Turbo up and a Sticker Book of every Grumbly he's cheered.

## Who's playing

The title screen has two big pups: **his** (plain Turbo) and **yours** (Turbo in sunglasses). Each player keeps their own mode, controls, stars, stickers and best results. The last player glows, so it's one tap for him.

| | Kid Mode (ages 4–6) | Dad Mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Controls | ◀ ▶ and a big jump button, or **Auto-Go** (Turbo walks by himself and stops at every gap; tap anywhere to jump) | A stick wherever your left thumb lands; JUMP, FIRE, and TURBO (when the meter is full). A thumb on the line between FIRE and JUMP presses both. |
| Shooting | The Pop-Pack fires by itself, only at Grumblies ahead, and aims for him | FIRE: forward, up-forward, up, or down in the air. Hold FIRE while standing still for a charged **Big Pop** that goes through Grumblies. Light aim assist. |
| Jumping | Floaty, forgiving timing, ledge assist. Keep holding jump while falling and his tail spins like a propeller. A jump from standing still at a gap carries him across. | Snappier and faster. Down + jump drops through bamboo. |
| Getting hurt | 3 hearts that refill. Brushing a Grumbly's edge doesn't count. Pits and the river cost nothing. | 3 hearts, plus one for each Heart Treat found (up to 5). A hit also costs a weapon level (never the weapon). A pit costs a heart. Brambles hurt. |
| Help | Falling into the same gap twice lays a rope bridge across it. After 3 hits in a section, Grumblies there slow down. After 8 s without getting further, Turbo barks and points the way, and the button to press glows. Leafy walls give way when he pushes them. | None unless you ask for it. |
| The level | Rope bridges over the widest gap; brambles are daisies | Brambles, and extra Grumblies |
| After the goal | A celebration: stars counting up, gems, and a sticker for each new kind of Grumbly. No score, ever. | Score, time against par, gems, best chain, badges, a medal (bronze, silver or gold) |
| Bosses | Fewer blocks, slower hops, no coconuts. Losing all hearts keeps the blocks already knocked off. | Four blocks, quicker hops, coconuts. Losing all hearts starts the fight over (it's short). |

## World 1: Jungle Jamboree

### The map

An island with a dotted path. Tap a stop and Turbo hops along the path to it, then the level starts. Each stop shows its picture, the gems found (three slots), a check when it's done and, in Dad Mode, its medal. Locked stops are grey with a padlock, and the next one to play pulses. A flag on a stop means there's a checkpoint to come back to: tapping it resumes there, and the small ↻ next to it starts over.

Corners: house (back to the players), pup (who's playing), book (Sticker Book), hat (Pup Closet), paw (the practice field), four tiles (all games, in the app), ⚙ (hold 2 s, settings).

- **Pup Closet:** hats, fur colors and Pop-Pack paint. Each unlocks at a total number of stars collected (all-time, never spent), shown under a padlock. A bar at the bottom fills toward the next one. Tap to wear; Turbo wears it everywhere, including on the map.
- **Sticker Book:** a slot for each kind of Grumbly and the boss. A cheered kind shows its happy sticker and how many times; one he hasn't met is a grey silhouette with a "?".

### The levels

**W1-1 "Banana Bounce"**, six sections, each starting at a checkpoint flag:

1. **Wake-up Meadow:** walking, jumping and the first Grumbly.
2. **Bounce Garden:** a bounce flower up to gem 1, the Bubble Cannon, a Hopper.
3. **Treetop Hop:** bamboo up to Star Spread, Flappers, the Shield Bubble.
4. **Cheer Party:** a Mega Bone (Giant Pup), then a crowd to fill the TURBO meter.
5. **Canopy Secret:** gem 3 behind a leafy wall you've just walked past. Gem 2 is up on the canopy, reached by bouncing off a bubbled Grumbly or a balloon.
6. **Party Finish:** the Party Popper goal.

**W1-2 "Shield Grove"**: Spitters on the path, Shield Bonkers in Lid Lane (Boing Balls hop over their lids), Frosty Ray on a ledge (frozen Grumblies are ice blocks to stand on or kick), Zoom Boots for a run, and a Heart Treat. The goal only drops in once **Big Bonker** is cheered: a big Bonker with a big lid who slams the ground and sends a wave along it (jump the wave). In Kid Mode three hits knock his lid off; in Dad Mode he keeps turning to face you, so get him from above or behind.

**W1-3 "Raft Rapids"**: Chargers in the field (Fizz Rockets here), Flappers over a bamboo bridge with a Magnet Collar, then the **raft**: step on and it carries Turbo down the river by itself. Jump the rocks and Flappers; falling in floats him back onto the raft. The far bank has more Chargers and a Heart Treat, then the waterfall goal.

**W1-B "Tiki Temple"**, the boss: temple steps with a heart, a Shield Bubble and Fizz Rockets, then **Tiki Tumble**, a stack of grumpy tiki blocks. It sleeps until Turbo walks in (the camera locks onto the arena). It crouches, hops toward him (its shadow shows where it will land) and lands with a wave to jump over. Then it's dizzy, its top block's eyes glow, and any hit knocks that block off. Each block gone makes it faster. Cheering the last block wins the **double jump** for good, in every level: jump again in the air.

### The Grumblies

- **Waddler:** patrols. In Dad Mode it spots you ("!") and hurries.
- **Hopper:** crouches, then hops. In Dad Mode it hops at you, small and big.
- **Flapper:** flies. In Dad Mode it wiggles its wings, then swoops.
- **Spitter:** puffs its cheeks, then spits a slow seed straight ahead. In Dad Mode it aims, in bursts of three, and your shots can pop the seeds.
- **Shield Bonker:** a pot lid in front. In Kid Mode two hits knock the lid off. In Dad Mode it turns to face you: get it from above or behind, or with Boing Balls.
- **Charger:** in Kid Mode it just walks fast. In Dad Mode it paws the ground, charges, and is dizzy (an opening) after hitting a wall.

Stomp a Grumbly to cheer it and bounce; hold jump to bounce higher. Cheers in a row build a chain and fill the **TURBO meter**: a rainbow, invincible, rapid-fire burst with faster music. It starts by itself in Kid Mode; in Dad Mode, press TURBO.

**Weapons:**
- **Pop Blaster:** always there.
- **Bubble Cannon:** traps a Grumbly in a bubble. Touch the bubble to pop it, or land on it for a big bounce.
- **Star Spread:** a fan of stars.
- **Boing Balls:** bouncy balls that follow the ground and drop onto shields from above.
- **Frosty Ray:** a short beam that freezes a Grumbly into an ice block for a few seconds. Stand on it, or walk into it to kick it across the ground (it cheers whatever it hits).
- **Fizz Rockets:** slow rockets that find the nearest Grumbly and burst.

Picking up the same weapon again levels it up (to 3). A different one swaps, and the old one floats nearby for 6 s to grab back. The nozzle on the Pop-Pack shows which weapon he has.

**Power-ups:**
- **Shield Bubble:** takes 3 hits in Kid Mode, 1 in Dad Mode.
- **Mega Bone:** Giant Pup for 7 s. He walks right through Grumblies and smashes leafy walls.
- **Zoom Boots:** 8 s of faster running and higher jumps.
- **Magnet Collar:** 15 s of stars flying to him.
- **Hearts.**
- **Heart Treats** (one in W1-2, one in W1-3): in Dad Mode, one more heart for good. In Kid Mode, all hearts filled.

**Dad Mode badges**, kept per level: **No-Hit**, **All Gems**, **Under Par** (finish within the par time; the timer is on the HUD), and **Paws Only** (finish without firing a shot). The results light the ones earned and ring the new ones.

## Buttons

| Button | Does |
| --- | --- |
| ⏸ (top left) | Pause: ▶ keep playing, ↻ back to the last checkpoint, house to the map, four tiles back to all the games (in the app) |
| ⏸ **held for 2 s**, or the ⚙ **held for 2 s** on any menu | Grown-up settings (a yellow ring fills while you hold) |

**Settings:**
- **Who's playing:** his pup or yours.
- **Mode:** Kid, Kid Auto-Go or Dad. Switching mid-level restarts from the last checkpoint.
- **Controls:** auto-fire and aim assist (Dad), left-handed, bigger buttons.
- **Volumes:** music, sounds and voice.
- **Comfort:** reduced motion, fewer flashes.
- **Grown-up tools:**
  - Show FPS, and the quality tier.
  - **Feel tuning:** sliders for every jump and speed number, live, with **Copy values** to paste into `js/game/tuning.js`.
  - The practice field.
  - The stress test.
  - Reset this player.

Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Space/Z jump, X/J fire, V TURBO, Esc pause, ` settings. A game controller works too: stick or d-pad, A jump, X/B fire, Y/LB TURBO, Menu pause. On-screen buttons hide while a keyboard or controller is in use. On the menus, the arrows, d-pad or stick move a yellow ring between the buttons (left and right walk the map's path), A or Space picks, and Esc or Menu goes back.

Saving is automatic and stays on the iPad. It saves at every checkpoint (closing the app mid-level comes back there, with what was collected), at every finish (with a backup), and whenever the app goes to the background.

## On the iPad: what to try

1. Run `npm run sync` in the repo root, then ▶ in Xcode.
2. **Your son:** his pup, Kid Mode (or Auto-Go if moving and jumping together is too much). Hands off, a session a day, until he's beaten Tiki Tumble (the goal is within 3 sessions). Watch the raft ride and the boss in particular. Afterwards, `?stats` (in a browser) or the log in settings shows where he spent time, got hit or fell.
3. **You:** your pup, Dad Mode. Go for all 9 gems, the golds and the badges, and say which felt unfair. If the jump feels off, change it in **Feel tuning** and send the **Copy values** text.
4. **Speed:** in settings, Show FPS. The river and the boss fight should hold about 16.7 ms.

## Run locally

No build step: it's plain static files and ES modules.

```sh
npm start   # python3 -m http.server 8890
npm test    # node --test
```

Open http://localhost:8890. URL flags:
- `?level=w1-1` (or `w1-2`, `w1-3`, `w1-b`, `greybox`) skips the menus. Add `&mode=kid|dad` to pick the player and `&auto=1` for Auto-Go.
- `?debug` shows the FPS overlay. It and `?test` both put the app on `window.__tq`.
- `?tune` opens settings.
- `?lab` shows every piece of art on a sheet; `?lab=2` (or a tap) shows World 1's later art: Pup Closet looks, the new Grumblies, Tiki Tumble, the river and the new items.
- `?stress` is the performance scene.
- `?stats` is the playtest log: per level and per section, time, hits, rescues and helps, newest first.
- `?q=high|medium|low` fixes the quality tier.
- `?card=1024` draws the Kid Games home screen picture, and `?icon=512` the icon.

## How it works

The rules never touch the screen. `js/game/`, `js/content/` and most of `js/engine/` have no DOM, so `node --test` runs them. The world talks to drawing, sound and effects through events (`cheer`, `jump`, `gem`, ...).

- **`js/content/`: data.**
  - `levels/` (ASCII sections; the glyphs are in `legend.js`; `kid`/`dad` overlays are explained in `game/level.js`).
  - `enemies.js` (each Grumbly with a Kid block and a Dad block: Dad Mode adds behaviors, never health).
  - `weapons.js`, `powerups.js` (also hearts, points and the double jump), `cosmetics.js` (the Pup Closet and its star prices), `sfx.js` (sounds as data), `songs.js` (the music, original: jungle, title and boss).
- **`js/game/`: the rules.**
  - `player.js`: running, jumping, coyote time, jump buffering, flutter, bounces, ledge and corner assists, Auto-Go, smart jump, rescue.
  - `world.js`: Grumblies, stomps, cheers and balloons, pickups, power-ups, TURBO, hearts, pits, Kid help, checkpoints, results, resume.
  - `enemies.js` (walk, hop, fly, spit, charge, shields, freezing, kicked ice), `boss.js` (Tiki Tumble's states: sleep, wake, crouch, hop, dizzy, hurt), `weapons.js` (firing, aim, the Big Pop, shots, swaps), `combo.js` (chain, score, TURBO meter, medals).
  - `level.js` also builds the raft (a one-way ride) and the boss arena.
  - `tuning.js`: every feel number for both modes.
  - `lint.js`: where each mode's jump can reach, for the level tests.
- **`js/engine/`:**
  - The fixed 60 Hz loop.
  - Collision.
  - The camera: looks ahead, snaps to platforms.
  - Touch controls, input (touch, keyboard, controllers).
  - Audio and the music sequencer, and the speaking voice (Mighty Mower's).
  - `save.js` (versioned, with a backup and recovery from a corrupt save).
  - The sprite cache.
- **`js/art/`: all art drawn in code.**
  - `turbo.js`: Turbo as a puppet of baked parts (head, ears on springs, eyes, mouth, legs, tail, Pop-Pack, a nozzle per weapon).
  - `grumblies.js`, `jungle.js` (auto-tiled ground, platforms, props, parallax), `items.js`, `style.js`.
- **`js/render/`:**
  - The game view (`renderer.js`) and the HUD and buttons (`ui.js`, on a second canvas at full sharpness, repainted only when something changes).
  - `player-view.js` (squash and stretch, faces), pooled particles.
  - Quality tiers that step down by themselves on a slow device.
- **`js/scenes/`:** title, map, Pup Closet, Sticker Book, play, pause, results, stress, lab. `menu.js` is their shared base: big round buttons, hold-to-confirm, and the keyboard and controller focus ring.
- **`js/dev/`:** the settings panel and the playtest log.

**The tests:**
- Jump heights and distances, coyote time and buffering.
- Every Grumbly behavior, weapon and power-up, and the Kid help.
- Saving, migration and corrupt-save recovery.
- Touch layouts, and moving around the menus with a controller.
- Two bots that must finish every level:
  - The "4-year-old with one button": Auto-Go plus a jump whenever Turbo waits, never running out of hearts.
  - A grown-up: runs, jumps gaps and Grumblies, pulses FIRE, rides the raft, dodges coconuts and shoots the dizzy boss.
- A reachability search that every level's goal and gems must pass, in both modes.
